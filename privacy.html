Overview

This policy explains what information CleanSlate ("the App") collects, how it's used, and your choices. CleanSlate is built by MirodaLab ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

CleanSlate is designed around fast, local note capture. By default, the notes you write are stored on your device. If a future version adds optional account creation, cloud backup, or sync, this section should be updated to describe what account information (such as an email address) that feature collects.

How We Use Information

Any notes you create are used only to power the App's own features — showing, searching, and organizing your notes. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data Storage & Security

Your notes live on your device unless you explicitly enable a sync or backup feature. If CleanSlate later adds cloud sync, describe here where that data is hosted and how it's protected.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Giscus for comments, which stores comments as GitHub Discussions under GitHub's own privacy policy. The App itself does not currently integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs — update this section if that changes.

Children's Privacy

CleanSlate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
